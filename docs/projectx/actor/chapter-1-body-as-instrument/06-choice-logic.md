# Choice Logic

## Purpose
Define what kinds of user choices should be rewarded in Chapter 1.

## Choices to reward
- noticing bodily state honestly
- choosing grounding before performance fantasy
- caring for breath and voice
- making small disciplined adjustments
- preferring readiness over appearance

## Choices to complicate
- rushing into performance mode
- chasing confidence without preparation
- ignoring physical signals
- treating the body as secondary to "talent"

## Resulting lesson
The actor's instrument becomes trustworthy when it is listened to, maintained, and used with discipline.

# Chapter 1 Choice Logic

## Stats

Presence:
Grounded physical and emotional availability.

Craft:
Technical preparation: voice, movement, script, camera awareness.

Character Connection:
Ability to understand and enter the character's inner life.

Resilience:
Ability to handle stress, comparison, uncertainty, and judgment.

## Chapter 1 choices

### Choice type: Voice and breath warm-up

Meaning:
The actor chooses craft and presence before appearance.

Stat effect:
- Craft +4
- Presence +3
- Resilience +1

Narrative direction:
The actor hums softly, releases jaw tension, feels breath settle under the ribs. The body becomes less decorative and more available.

### Choice type: Appearance and outfit check

Meaning:
The actor chooses camera readiness and professional presentation.

Stat effect:
- Presence +3
- Craft +1
- Resilience -1 if done from anxiety
- Resilience +1 if done from professionalism

Narrative direction:
The actor checks skin, hair, outfit, and face condition. The mirror can become either a tool or a trap.

### Choice type: Script review first

Meaning:
The actor chooses character and story before physical preparation.

Stat effect:
- Character Connection +4
- Craft +2
- Presence -1 if body is ignored

Narrative direction:
The actor reads the scene while still half-awake. A line begins to feel less like text and more like a wound someone has been carrying.

### Choice type: Scroll social media

Meaning:
The actor seeks external validation or distraction.

Stat effect:
- Stress +3
- Resilience -2
- Presence -2

Narrative direction:
Other people's faces, announcements, and success stories crowd the actor's mind before the day has even begun.

### Choice type: Body stretch and movement

Meaning:
The actor prepares physical freedom.

Stat effect:
- Presence +4
- Craft +2
- Resilience +1

Narrative direction:
The spine wakes first, then the shoulders, then the breath. The actor remembers that emotion needs space to move.
