# Nervous System and Presence

## Key truth
Presence is not mystical. It is often what the body feels like when it is regulated enough to respond truthfully.

## What to teach
- anxiety changes timing, voice, and gaze
- regulation creates availability
- stillness can be active
- attention is felt by scene partners and by camera

## Story direction
This chapter should let the user notice:
- when they brace
- when they rush
- when they hide inside performance
- when they actually arrive

## Useful line of thought
An actor cannot always control emotion.
They can often shape the conditions that let emotion move honestly.


# Nervous System and Presence

An actor needs to stay open while being watched.

Before an audition or shoot, the actor may feel:
- anxiety
- comparison
- excitement
- pressure
- fear of rejection
- desire to be chosen
- fear of not being enough

Presence means the actor can return to breath, body, and attention.

Useful grounding techniques:
- Feel feet or body weight
- Breathe slowly
- Relax jaw and shoulders
- Notice the room without judgment
- Return attention to the character's need
- Listen instead of trying to impress

The actor's challenge:
"Can I be emotionally available without losing myself?"

The chapter should show that presence is trained. It is not just natural charisma.
