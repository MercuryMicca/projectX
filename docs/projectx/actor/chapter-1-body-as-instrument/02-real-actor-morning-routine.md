# Real Actor Morning Routine

## Focus
Ground the chapter in believable actor habits before rehearsal, class, self-tape, or set call.

## Useful realities
- Sleep quality affects memory, responsiveness, and emotional range.
- Hydration matters for voice and physical regulation.
- Light mobility work helps release unnecessary tension.
- Quiet preparation often matters more than dramatic motivation.

## Morning beats to draw from
1. Waking and noticing the body's condition
2. Water, tea, or a simple vocal-friendly first ritual
3. Gentle stretching, spinal mobility, or breath work
4. Checking face, posture, and energy with practical honesty
5. Mentally arriving before any performance demand begins

## Scene texture
Keep details concrete:
- dry mouth
- heavy shoulders
- cool floor
- steam from tea
- first sound of the voice in the room


# Actor Morning Routine

Actors often begin important days by checking their body, voice, energy, and mental state.

Useful morning details:
- Wake up early before call time or audition time.
- Drink water to support voice and skin.
- Avoid foods that cause bloating, throat irritation, or low energy.
- Do light stretching to release neck, jaw, shoulders, spine, and hips.
- Warm up the voice gently.
- Check face swelling, skin condition, hair, and outfit.
- Review the script or sides.
- Avoid overusing the phone before performance because comparison and distraction can increase anxiety.
- Prepare bag: script, water, snacks, makeup/skincare, comfortable shoes, audition outfit, charger.

The morning should feel like preparation, not luxury.

The actor is asking:
"Is my body available today?"
"Is my voice awake?"
"Can I stay open under pressure?"
